About

During my bachelor's in Chemistry, a roommate once photographed me asleep in a thinking pose. "See?" she laughed. "You were thinking even while sleeping."

Maybe it was a coincidence. But I genuinely try to be mindful of every aspect of life as much as I can, and to read books that challenge my assumptions and teach me about systematic biases.

I'm a computational chemist interested in multiscale simulations and physics-informed ML — applying data-driven methods to accelerate materials discovery and catalysis. Beyond the lab, my interests include cycling and reading: philosophy, to learn the essence of arguments; psychology, to understand what moves people; and sociology and politics, to stay rooted in reality.

I challenge stereotypes fiercely. That doesn't mean I think I'm always right — if you have a counterargument, I'll make room for it. Either it expands my understanding or it sharpens how I express my own. Both are worth having.

Coffee in hand, mid-thought — as usual.
