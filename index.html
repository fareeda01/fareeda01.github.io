About

During my bachelor's in Chemistry, a roommate once photographed me asleep in a thinking pose and showed it to me in the morning. "See?" she laughed. "You were thinking even while sleeping."

Maybe it was a coincidence. But I genuinely try to be mindful of every aspect of life. I'm a computational chemist interested in multiscale simulations and physics-informed ML — applying data-driven methods to accelerate materials discovery and catalysis.

Along with that, I challenge stereotypes fiercely. That doesn't mean I think I'm always right — if you have a counterargument, I'll make room for it. Either it expands my understanding or it sharpens how I express my own. Both are worth having.

Coffee in hand, mid-thought — as usual.
