About

During my bachelor's in Chemistry, a roommate once photographed me asleep — sitting upright in a thinking pose. "See?" she laughed. "You were thinking even while sleeping."

Maybe it was a coincidence. But I do try to stay mindful of every aspect of life as much as I can.

I'm a chemist by training, yet I think specialization — though important — is often overrated. Creativity in science can't grow in isolation when we live in such a deeply interconnected world. That's why my interests reach well beyond the lab, into philosophy, psychology, sociology, and politics.

I challenge stereotypes fiercely. That doesn't mean I think I'm always right — if you have a counterargument, I'll make room for it. Either it expands my understanding or it sharpens how I express my own. Both are worth having.
